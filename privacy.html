Privacy Policy

We collect zero data. Seriously.

Apps Covered

This privacy policy applies to all Adaptive Human mobile applications, including:

  • Video Poker Edge - Video poker strategy trainer

What We Don't Collect

Our apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Period.

  • No accounts or sign-ups required
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No network connections to external servers
  • No third-party data sharing
  • No crash reporting that includes personal data

We have no servers. We receive no data from you whatsoever.

How Your Data Is Stored

All game progress, statistics, and settings are stored locally on your device only using standard app storage (AsyncStorage on mobile platforms).

This data never leaves your device. When you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Updates to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices (we don't anticipate this), we will update this policy and the "Last Updated" date below.
